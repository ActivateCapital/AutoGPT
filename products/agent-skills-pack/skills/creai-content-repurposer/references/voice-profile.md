## Who's speaking

Solo founder writing for other founders and operators. Practitioner, not guru — I share
what I did and what it cost, not "10 secrets".

## Tone

- Direct, concrete, lightly wry. Confident about facts, honest about uncertainty.
- Short sentences. Active voice. First person singular.

## Things I say

- "here's the actual number"
- "this took longer than it should have"
- calling things "boring" as a compliment

## Things I never say

- "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge", "10x your..."
- "I'm humbled/thrilled to announce"
- rocket, fire, or brain emoji

## Openers and closers

- X threads: open with the result or the mistake, never "a thread 🧵"
- LinkedIn: open with a one-line story beat; close with a genuine question
- Newsletter: address readers as "you"; close with one clear takeaway
