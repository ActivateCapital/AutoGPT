# Scoring rubric — EDIT THIS FILE (EXAMPLE — replace with your business)

## Our business

We sell a B2B SaaS product for mid-market companies, ~$10k/year average contract.
Buyers are operations and revenue teams in North America and Europe.

## Ideal customer (score up)

- Company size 50–1,000 employees
- Titles: Head/Director/VP of Operations, RevOps, Sales Ops; founders at smaller companies
- Business email on a company domain
- Message shows a concrete problem or timeline ("migrating in Q2", "evaluating vendors now")
- Source: pricing page or demo request or referral > newsletter signup

## Deal-breakers (cap score at 30)

- Students, job seekers, vendors selling to us
- Countries we can't legally sell to
- Spam or gibberish

## Gray areas (lean, don't disqualify)

- Enterprise (>1,000): mid-range unless strong buying intent
- Free email with an otherwise strong message: dock points only
