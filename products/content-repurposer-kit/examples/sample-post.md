# We cut our onboarding time from 14 days to 3 — by deleting features

When we launched our analytics tool, onboarding took new customers an average of 14
days. We assumed the fix was more: more tooltips, more checklists, more webinars. We
built all three. Onboarding went from 14 days to 15.

The thing that worked was subtraction. We watched 20 onboarding sessions and found that
80% of the confusion came from three power-user features new users touched in week one:
custom dashboards, the formula editor, and workspace permissions. None of them mattered
until month two.

So we hid them. New accounts now start with four screens instead of eleven. The formula
editor unlocks after the first ten reports; permissions unlock when a second teammate
joins. We braced for complaints from power users. We got two emails — both answered with
"click Settings → Advanced", both satisfied.

Results after 90 days:

- Median onboarding: 3.2 days (from 14)
- Week-one activation rate: 61% (from 34%)
- Support tickets from new accounts: down 44%

The lesson we keep relearning: the cost of a feature isn't building it, it's every new
user who has to walk past it. Your onboarding doesn't need a map. It needs fewer rooms.
