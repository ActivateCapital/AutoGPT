# Channel guides — tweak to taste

## X thread (5–8 tweets)

- Tweet 1 is the hook: the result, the mistake, or the contrarian claim. No "🧵", no
  "a thread on...".
- One idea per tweet; each tweet must make sense if quoted alone.
- Under 280 characters per tweet, hard limit.
- Last tweet: the single takeaway, plus (only if the source has one) a link back to the
  full piece.
- No hashtags.

## LinkedIn post (150–300 words)

- First line earns the "see more" click: a story beat or a sharp claim, not a greeting.
- Short paragraphs (1–2 sentences), generous line breaks.
- Concrete numbers and specifics from the source over adjectives.
- Close with one genuine question to the reader. At most one hashtag.

## Newsletter section (100–200 words)

- Written to "you", as if to one subscriber who trusts us.
- Frame: why this matters to the reader → the meat → one clear takeaway or action.
- Can reference "this week I..." framing; warmer than the other channels.

## YouTube description (80–150 words + chapters)

- First two lines carry the search intent: what the viewer will learn, plainly —
  they're what shows before "show more".
- Then 3–6 chapter lines in `MM:SS Topic` form. If the source has no timestamps, infer
  a sensible chapter list from its structure and mark it "(chapters approximate)".
- End with one line about who the video is for. No hashtag spam (two max).
