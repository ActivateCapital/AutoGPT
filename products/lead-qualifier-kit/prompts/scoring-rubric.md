# Scoring rubric — EDIT THIS FILE

Describe your business and ideal customer below in plain English. The agent applies this
rubric to every lead. The sharper you make it, the better the scores. Replace the example
with your own.

## Our business

We sell a B2B SaaS product for mid-market companies. Average contract value is around
$10k/year. We sell primarily to operations and revenue teams in North America and Europe.

## Ideal customer (score these up)

- Company size 50–1,000 employees
- Titles: Head/Director/VP of Operations, RevOps, Sales Ops; founders at smaller companies
- Uses a business email on a company domain
- Message describes a concrete problem or timeline ("we're migrating in Q2", "evaluating
  vendors now")
- Inbound source: pricing page, demo request, or referral beats newsletter signup

## Deal-breakers (cap the score at 30)

- Students, job seekers, or vendors trying to sell to us
- Countries we cannot legally sell to
- Obvious spam or gibberish

## Gray areas (use judgment)

- Enterprise (>1,000 employees): possible but slow — score mid-range unless the message
  shows strong buying intent
- Free email address with an otherwise strong message: dock points, don't disqualify
